# JobGenesis social demo video

## Goal
Create a polished **vertical 9:16, 55–60 second** founder-story launch video for LinkedIn, Instagram Reels, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts. The pitch begins with a student repeatedly struggling in real interviews, shows the frustration of not knowing what went wrong, and reveals JobGenesis as the solution.

## Creative direction
- **Style:** cinematic generated scenes blended with real JobGenesis product footage, not a generic screen recording.
- **Mood:** personal student struggle → frustration and uncertainty → discovery → focused practice → confidence and proof.
- **Brand:** use JobGenesis’s deep ink, warm paper, teal accent, Space Grotesk-style display type, and restrained premium look.
- **Brand boundary:** show only JobGenesis branding. Do not include or mention Lovable in the video, captions, narration, screens, metadata, or end card.
- **Voice:** clear, confident English AI narration; concise burned-in captions for silent autoplay.
- **Music:** modern, focused pulse with subtle interface sound accents, mixed below narration.

## Storyboard and pitch
1. **Personal hook — 0–8s**  
   Generated scene: a student waits outside an interview, enters nervously, then leaves disappointed. Quick cuts suggest the cycle repeating.  
   Narration: “I’m a student. I prepared for interviews, watched endless videos, and still froze when the real questions started.”

2. **The real problem — 8–16s**  
   Generated scene: the student reviews notes late at night, replaying the interview without knowing which answers failed.  
   Narration: “The hardest part wasn’t rejection. It was not knowing what I did wrong—or how to improve before the next one.”

3. **JobGenesis reveal — 16–22s**  
   Cinematic transition from the student’s resume into the JobGenesis product.  
   Narration: “So I built JobGenesis: an AI interviewer that starts with your actual resume.”

4. **Resume intelligence — 22–29s**  
   Real product footage: upload and JobGenesis analysis experience.  
   Narration: “Upload your resume, choose the role you want, and it builds a personal interview around your experience.”

5. **Adaptive voice interview — 29–42s**  
   Generated over-the-shoulder interview moment transitions into real product footage showing voice interaction, microphone choice, role selection, and adaptive questioning.  
   Narration: “Speak naturally. It questions your projects, follows up when an answer is vague, and tests the skills the job actually needs.”

6. **Evidence and improvement — 42–53s**  
   Real product footage: report summary, role fit, strengths, and growth areas.  
   Narration: “Afterward, you see what held up, what needs work, and how well your answers match the role—so the next interview isn’t another guess.”

7. **Confident payoff — 53–60s**  
   Generated scene: the same student enters the next interview calm and prepared, followed by a brief JobGenesis scorecard reveal.  
   Narration: “I built the practice tool I wished I had. JobGenesis—know your resume, prove your skills.”  
   End frame: JobGenesis name, tagline, and the project URL.

## Production
- Generate a cohesive recurring student character across the struggle, late-night reflection, practice, and confident ending scenes.
- Capture only genuine JobGenesis screens and interactions; no fabricated scores, claims, or analysis.
- Edit generated scenes, product footage, motion typography, captions, narration, music, and sound effects into one 1080×1920 MP4.
- Keep key text inside platform-safe areas so controls do not cover it.
- Use quick cuts for the hook, smoother camera movement through the interview, and a resolved branded ending.

## Quality checks
- Verify the full video, narration, captions, music balance, spelling, and platform-safe framing.
- Inspect representative frames from every scene for clipping, warped generated details, unreadable UI, or invented product behavior.
- Confirm the final duration, resolution, audio track, file size, and MP4 playback before delivery.

## Deliverable
One downloadable vertical master MP4 suitable for LinkedIn, Instagram Reels, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts.
